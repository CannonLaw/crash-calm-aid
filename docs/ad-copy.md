# Ad Copy

Drafts of Google Ads Responsive Search Ad (RSA) copy for the three audience
segments. RSA format: up to 15 headlines (30 chars each), up to 4
descriptions (90 chars each), per ad. Google rotates and tests
combinations.

Every variant below is **draft, not approved**. Copy decisions touch
Cannon Law brand voice and PI advertising compliance; the firm reviews
before anything goes live.

All numbers, addresses, and the phone number `(970) 471-7170` come from
the existing app disclaimer (`src/pages/CrashResponse/Home.tsx:141-145`)
and are confirmed.

Compliance notes that apply to every variant:

- No statements of expected outcome ("we'll win your case," dollar
  guarantees, etc.). Colorado RPC 7.1 / 7.2.
- "No win, no fee" wording is used by the firm already (see app
  disclaimer); acceptable.
- The disclaimer in the PDF report and on the Home page remains the
  legally-required hedge; ad creative does not need to repeat it but
  should not contradict it.
- Soft / informational tone preferred over aggressive solicitation per the
  brief.
